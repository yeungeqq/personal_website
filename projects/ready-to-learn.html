Ready to Learn

A classroom readiness platform connecting student check-ins with teacher and school-level views.

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Spring Boot
  • PostgreSQL

The idea

Ready to Learn centers on a classroom workflow: collect student readiness ratings and make them useful to teachers. The documented platform connects check-in sessions, classroom management, and reporting across teacher and administrator roles.

Project scope

  • Start session-based readiness check-ins and share a student access link.
  • Organize schools, classes, and student records.
  • View readiness trends through teacher and administrator dashboards.
  • Export reports in PDF or Excel formats.

Technical approach

The React and TypeScript frontend works with a Spring Boot REST backend and PostgreSQL. The project separates classroom interactions from administrative views and includes technical, testing, and handover documentation alongside the application.

Scope & limitations

This overview describes the repository's documented scope, not verified school adoption or educational outcomes. A public demonstration should use synthetic student records. Individual team responsibilities are not attributed here because they have not been confirmed.
